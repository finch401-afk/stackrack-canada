Ask a Question
Do stacking racks need to be anchored to the floor?

No. StackRack units are free-standing and need no anchoring, uprights or beams. Loaded racks are stacked on top of each other with a forklift, so you can move, add or remove racks whenever your inventory or layout changes.

How much weight can each rack hold, and how high can they stack?

Load capacity of up to 2,000 kg per rack is available, and racks can be stacked up to five levels high. The right capacity and number of levels depend on the model, your product and your building, so we confirm both for your configuration before you order.

Can the racks be used in freezers and cold storage?

Yes. Our cold storage racks are designed for frozen food, meat, produce and refrigerated distribution facilities. Tell us your operating temperature and we will recommend a suitable structure and finish.

What happens to the racks when they are empty?

Empty racks can be dismantled, folded or nested, depending on the model, so they take up only a fraction of the floor space of a loaded rack. This makes them easy to store during slow seasons or return to a central location.

Can you make racks in custom sizes?

Yes. Dimensions, load capacity, finish, colour and structure can all be adapted. Send us your product size, weight and handling method, and we will propose a design that fits, including special racks for tires, glass, doors, batteries and long materials.

What information do you need for a quote or layout?

Your warehouse dimensions and clear height (or a floor plan), product size, load weight, approximate quantity and forklift type. With that we can prepare a free preliminary layout, capacity estimate and quotation.

Do you deliver outside Calgary?

Yes. We are based in Calgary and support projects across Canada. Delivery cost and timing depend on quantity and destination, and are included in your quotation.

How much do the racks cost?

Pricing depends on the rack size, load capacity, finish and order quantity. Submit the quote form or call 825-488-5555 and we will send a quotation for your project. You can also download our catalogue (PDF) to compare models.

Is stacking loaded racks safe?

Racks are designed to be stacked within their rated capacity and number of levels. As with any storage system, floor capacity, aisle widths, sprinkler clearances and safe operating procedures for your site must be confirmed by qualified professionals before installation.
